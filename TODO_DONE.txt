Trade Ship (moved from TODO_TOBECHECKED.txt — implemented and tested)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A trade ship can establish a trade route from a city with a harbour to a city with another harbour,
and then the ship uses GoTo (pathfinding) to drive between the two cities automatically.
Everytime it was in one city of your civilisation and then reaches another city of your
civilisation or of another civ, the players get money.
One ship owner civ. gets 80% of the following calculation and the owner of the target city 20%:
[(1+F*X)*(5*(P-10))+100+Cap)* (Minimum ([T*50%], [D*70%]))*20%/100]
F is 150% (1.5) if it is a Foreign Trade Route, 0 if domestic
X = 2*Enemy Civ., 1*Your Own, 1.2 Friend, Peace Agreement NOT war.
P is the population of the city Getting the trade route
T is the population of the City the Trade Route is With
D is the Distance to the City
Cap is 25 if one of the cities is connected to the Capital, 0 if not
[] indicates rounding down
If this ship gets sunk by another ship then the owner civ. that sunk the ship gets 100% of what
the formula would have resulted. Assume: the target city in the formula is the ship,
D is the distance to the ship, Pop is the population of the city the sunk ship came from.
