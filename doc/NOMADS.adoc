= Nomads — The Opening Visitors

> *Engine:* `src/game/engine/NomadManager.ts`, `src/game/engine/GameEngine.ts`,
> `src/game/engine/TurnManager.ts` (round hook)
> *Data:* `src/data/NomadConstants.ts`
> *UI:* `src/components/ui/gamemodals/AudienceModal.tsx`,
> `src/components/ui/GameModals.tsx`
> *Tests:* `tests/nomads.test.ts` (13)
> *i18n:* `nomad.*` keys in all 6 languages

---

== 1. What happens

Once per **single-human** game, shortly after the first city stands (round
≥ 4, or round ≥ 12 if no city yet), a neutral horde camps 9–13 tiles from
the player's court: two warriors and a scout, owned by `BARBARIAN_CIV_ID`,
fortified, revealed to the player on sight (`NOMAD_SIGHTED`).

Two rounds later the envoy stands at the gate and the **parley** opens —
the audience screen with its own speaker (the Nomad Chieftain) and four
doors:

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
|Door |Effect

|Buy their knowledge
|One technology the court lacks, for **half its raw research cost**
(clamped 25–100 gold). A broke court receives it free ("the road gives").
The price and the technology are whatever the BUTTON promised — never
re-rolled while the modal is open.

|Accept their maps
|`revealArea` — a radius-10 swath around the camp is revealed to the
player. No other game source reveals fog without walking it.

|Hire the horde
|Pay the units' build cost × 1.1; the three neutral units convert to the
player's colors (un-fortified, ready next turn).

|Pass in peace
|They leave after 6 rounds instead of 20.
|===

Whichever deal was struck (or none), the **camp remains a race**: from 8
rounds after the parley, every eligible AI court may hire it (chance scales
with the leader's diplomacy; `NOMAD_HIRED` is announced). If nobody does,
the camp wanders off into the fog after 20 rounds (`NOMAD_DEPARTED`).

A **second parley** fires when the player's unit stands at the camp after
the first answer — hire or farewell only.

== 2. Rules and boundaries

* **Single human seat only.** Hotseat would have to pick whose court
  receives them; all-AI games have nobody to talk to. Both are skipped.
* **Neutral, not hostile.** Civ id `-1` (with the barbarians): contact
  recording skips negative ids, no civ is at war with the camp, the AI
  never targets it — scenery with a contract, not a combatant.
* **Session-only.** Nothing is persisted; a loaded game simply has no
  nomads (v1).
* **Dismissal is not refusal.** Closing the audience without answering
  leaves the parley open: it re-offers when the player stands at the camp
  (3-round cooldown), never every round.
* The parley is a **blocking dialog** — `e2e/game.spec.ts`
  `BLOCKING_DIALOGS` dismisses it with *Pass in peace* so tests keep pace.

== 3. Integration points

[cols="2,3", options="header"]
|===
|Piece |Where

|Round hook
|`TurnManager.advanceTurn` new-round block, beside the team coordinator

|Audience
|`AudienceNotice.kind: 'nomad'` + `speaker` + `choices` + `leadKey`;
resolved through `GameEngine.nomadManager.resolveParley(id)` via
`GameModals.handleAudienceChoice`

|Events
|`NOMAD_SIGHTED` / `NOMAD_PARLEY` / `NOMAD_RESOLVED` / `NOMAD_HIRED` /
`NOMAD_DEPARTED` → `EngineEventHandlers` (localized toasts + store sync)

|Fog
|`GameEngine.revealArea(col, row, radius, viewerId)` — the viewer
parameter exists because the round hook runs before `activePlayer` flips;
the store refresh now follows "viewer is a human seat" instead of the old
`activePlayer === 0` assumption
|===

== 4. Tests

`tests/nomads.test.ts` — trigger and spawn (distance, landmass, neutral
id, fortification, no-city fallback, hotseat/AI skip), the four doors and
their promised terms, the broken court's free gift, the maps reveal, the
color swap on hire, the `noGold` refusal, the departure timer, the AI hire
race (seeded roll), and the dismissal/proximity re-offer.
