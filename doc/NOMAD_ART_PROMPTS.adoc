= Nomad Clan Art — Generation Prompts

> The audience screen shows one big portrait per nomad clan
> (`public/assets/nomads/<id>.png`, rendered at ~320px wide with
> `image-rendering: pixelated`, mood-coloured frame around it).
> Until a file exists, the dialog falls back to the clan's emoji, so images
> can be added one at a time. **Generate at 512×640 (portrait), PNG,
> background baked dark** — the stage behind it is `#0d1319`.

== Shared style block (prepend to every prompt)

----
Pixel art, 32-bit JRPG dialogue-portrait style, bust framing of a nomad
leader facing the viewer, three-quarter view, head and shoulders filling
the frame. Chunky readable pixels, limited earthy palette with one
signature accent colour per clan, strong rim light from the left, plain
very dark background (#0d1319) with a soft vignette. Weathered face,
expressive eyes, cloth and leather rendered with visible pixel clusters.
No text, no watermark, no border, no modern objects. 512x640 portrait.
----

== 1. The Sandborne — traders (`sandborne.png`)

Signature accent: warm amber/ochre. Mood: friendly, shrewd.

----
[basic style block]
A smiling middle-aged desert merchant-chief, sun-weathered grin, ochre
head cloth pinned with a copper coin, braided beard with small trade
trinkets woven in, layered road-worn robes in amber and sand tones.
Behind one shoulder, softly out of focus: the silhouette of a laden
wagon and a camel's hump against dunes at dusk. Warm amber rim light,
 inviting and sly.
----

== 2. The Ironhoof — proud warriors (`ironhoof.png`)

Signature accent: iron grey with blood-red. Mood: proud, unyielding.

----
[basic style block]
A stern steppe warlord, jaw set, eyes narrowed, no smile. Black iron
lamellar pauldrons over a red deel, a thin scar across one cheekbone,
hair in two warrior braids with iron rings. Behind the shoulder: the
blurred head of a steppe horse and a tasseled lance. Cold iron-grey
light with a red edge highlight, unyielding and proud.
----

== 3. The Greywind — mystics / path-finders (`greywind.png`)

Signature accent: cold moonlit blue with silver. Mood: enigmatic,

----
[basic style block]
An androgynous elderly path-finder seer, hood half-falling, calm
