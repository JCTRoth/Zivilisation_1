= AI vs AI — Micro-Error Analysis & Fix Report
:author: opencode (automated AI analysis)
:date: 2026-09-27
:status: analysis + fixes applied
:scope: src/game/engine/AI/*, src/game/engine/AutoProduction.ts

== 1. Executive summary

A fresh 5-minute AI-vs-AI session (587 rounds, 21 969 log lines,
3 113 moves, 91 combats) was analysed at the micro level — per-unit,
per-turn decisions rather than strategy. Three concrete micro-bugs were
found in the code and fixed:

. `isUnitCapExhausted` used `Math.max` where its own comment demanded the
   most *conservative* cap — the AI built armies it could not pay for and
   disbanded **92 units** in one game.
. `computeAggression` assigned a phantom army ratio of **5.0** when no
   enemy had been spotted, inflating the aggression score to an average of
   **88.5** against a trigger threshold of 50 — the AI was near-permanently
   aggressive on zero intelligence.
. `AIStrategySelector` had no hysteresis — civs flip-flopped between
   strategies **15 times** in one game as war status and city counts
   oscillated.

== 2. Methodology

`scripts/run-ai-analysis-session.mjs` played a 300 s headless game and
exported the progression CSV + map JSON. The JSONL log was then aggregated
for micro-signals: skips, fortifies, path failures, disbands, fallback
moves, strategy changes, aggression scores, and per-civ combat records.

== 4. Fixes applied

=== 4.1 [HIGH] `isUnitCapExhausted` — optimistic cap caused 92 disbands
`AutoProduction.ts:1204-1220`

The comment said *"take the MOST CONSERVATIVE credible cap"* but the code
was `Math.max(cityCount, aiSustainableUnits ?? econSustainableUnits)` —
the most **optimistic** cap. When both the AI model and the engine model
answered, the AI's optimistic tax projection (100 % tax floor) overrode the
engine's own upkeep math. The AI queued phalanx after phalanx, the treasury
went negative, the engine auto-disbanded the surplus, and the loop repeated
all game.

Fix: when both models exist, take `Math.min` — the smaller, credible figure.

=== 4.2 [HIGH] `computeAggression` — phantom 5× advantage on zero intelligence
`AIAggression.ts:154-160`

When `enemyArmyStrength === 0` (no enemy spotted) the ratio defaulted to
`5` — a guaranteed "army advantage" +15 on *zero information*. Combined
with +20 "cities secured" and +10 "no border pressure", the baseline hit
45 before personality, averaging **88.5** against the 50 trigger.

Fix: unknown enemy strength ⇒ neutral ratio `1.0`.

=== 4.3 [MEDIUM] `AIStrategySelector` — no hysteresis, 15 strategy flips
`AIStrategySelector.ts`

Re-evaluation ran every 10 rounds and switched on a 1-point lead. War
status alone swings `military_expansion` +20 vs `balanced_growth` −15, so
every declaration / peace flip-flopped the strategy.

Fix: a challenger must beat the current strategy by `STRATEGY_SWITCH_MARGIN`
(6 points) before switching.

== 6. Known minor micro-errors (not fixed)

* "No affordable step" (49×) — genuine edge case: a unit ringed by tiles it
  cannot pay for. Blacklist + skip is a reasonable response; a full
  cost-aware A* is a larger change.
* "Already at target" (180×) — by design: garrisoned units holding position.
* Fallback moves (98×) — no-path best-effort; acceptable.
