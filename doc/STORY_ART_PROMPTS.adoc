= Story Screen Art — Generation Prompts

> Full-screen illustrated moments (the chronicle). Each image is shown as
> a **full-bleed cover hero** (`GameModal artLayout="cover"`), roughly
> 64vh tall on the story cards and 52vh inside the city dialog,
> pixel-scaled (`image-rendering: pixelated`) over a `#0b0f14` stage.
>
> **All images are optional.** A missing file degrades to the card's own
> glyph stage — generate them one at a time, in any order. The nomad clan
> portraits are a separate set: `doc/NOMAD_ART_PROMPTS.adoc`.
>
> **Deliver as WebP, 16:9 landscape — 1536×864 (or 1024×576) — named exactly
> as the table says, under `public/assets/story/`.**

== Shared style block (prepend to every prompt)

----
Cinematic scene style, WIDE 16:9 landscape
composition. Chunky readable pixels, limited earthy palette with ONE
signature accent colour per scene, strong directional light,
dark vignetted edges that melt into a very dark
blue-black background (#0b0f14). One clear subject in the middle third,
readable as a thumbnail. Mood over detail. No text, no watermark, no UI
elements, no modern objects. 1536x864 landscape WebP.
----

=== 2. `horde_conquest.webp` — the barbarians take a city

Signature accent: ember orange against smoke-grey. Mood: dread.

----
[basic style block]
A walled city under storm at dusk: the gate splintered, a barbarian horde
surging through it with torches and crude banners, thick black smoke
rolling up past the walls, one tower burning and casting ember light over
the attackers. Defenders' abandoned shields on the ground. Ember orange
against smoke-grey; the horde's crimson banner as the single sharp
accent.
----

=== 3. `nomad_migration.webp` — the nomads join them

Signature accent: dusk violet with warm lantern amber. Mood: eerie
companionship, movement.

----
[basic style block]
A nomad column on the move at dusk: covered wagons and mounted riders
filed along a road toward a captured walled city on the horizon (its
smoldering gate still faintly lit), tents and baggage in the foreground
already half-pitched on the plain. Warm lantern amber on the wagons
against a dusk-violet sky; the city's ember glow answering in the
distance. Two banners, one crimson one ochre, moving the same way.
----

== City chronicle (CityEventModal — same cover treatment)

=== 4. `city_founded.webp` — a city is founded

Signature accent: fresh morning green + gold. Mood: hopeful beginning.

----
[basic style block]
Settlers raising a first settlement at dawn: palisade stakes going up,
a banner being planted on a low mound beside a river bend, tents and a
half-built granary, forest and open plain beyond. Fresh green and morning
gold; a lone founding banner as the sharp accent. Clean, hopeful, empty
sky.
----

=== 5. `city_triumph.webp` — you capture a city

Signature accent: cool steel + dust gold. Mood: triumph.

----
[basic style block]
The moment of conquest from the victors' side: armour-clad soldiers
planting a new banner atop a captured gatehouse while the old banner lies
lowered on the stones below, city walls receding into a dust-hazed plaza,
morning light raking across the wall tops. Cool steel blues and dust gold;
the freshly planted banner the sharpest silhouette in the frame.
----

=== 6. `city_flames.webp` — a city is razed or lost

Signature accent: fire red against night. Mood: ruin.

----
[basic style block]
A city burning at night, seen from outside the walls: flames licking
broken towers, a fallen gate, sparks drifting across a black sky, one
abandoned cart and scattered belongings in the foreground road. Deep night
blues with fire red and orange as the only warm colours; ash in the air.
----

=== 7. `city_joined.webp` — a city joins you willingly

Signature accent: warm amber + open gate. Mood: welcome.

----
[basic style block]
A peaceful city gate standing wide open at golden hour, townsfolk
gathered before it with an offered banner and bread and salt on a cloth,
your colours already being raised on the wall beside their own. Warm
amber light, open composition, no weapons drawn. Hospitality as the
subject.
----

Wonders need no prompts of their own: `WonderCompletedModal` shows the
wonder's OWN shipped picture (`public/assets/wonders/*.jpg`) through the
same huge cover treatment. A failed load anywhere is non-fatal — the
shell falls back to the moment's glyph on the gradient stage, and the
text tells the story either way.
