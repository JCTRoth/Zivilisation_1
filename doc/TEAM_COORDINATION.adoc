== 1. What was built

Teams v1 (fixed alliances) made teammates *safe* from each other. This work makes
them *coordinated* — a stateless `TeamCoordinator` runs once per round (from
`TurnManager.advanceTurn`'s new-round block, before any member's turn) and
recomputes soft directives that member AIs consult while deciding:

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
|Phase |Behaviour

|0 — Seam
|`TeamCoordinator`: per-round, stateless, FFA-inert (every query collapses to
"no directive" when `!teamManager.enabled`), nothing persisted — save format
stays v5.

|1 — Coordinated scouting
|Frontier region claims: every unexplored-for-the-team frontier cell is claimed
by exactly one explorer (nearest land scout for land, nearest hull for water,
ties by unit id). Three scout choosers consult `shouldYield` as a *preference
with fallback* — a scout can never starve behind a teammate's region. POI
registry (publish/get/age-pruning) for later consumers.

|2 — Coordinated attacking
|One joint primary enemy city per team war, scored by value / proximity to the
team's forces / team-wide retaliation memory (a member's captured city steers
the whole team at the capturer for 15 rounds). Stable while marching, rotates
on capture/owner-change/age. Members receive it as a `teamFocus` in
`planBulkAttack` (`TEAM_FOCUS_BONUS = 90`), injected into their known targets
with land-then-ferry reachability. Unreachable focus never causes re-plan churn;
strength gating and the critical-threat defense veto still override.

|3 — Mutual defense
|Each member's cities are assessed per round from the OWNER's perspective
(garrison = owner + stacked teammates; teammate units filtered out of threat
samples, including stale sightings on their tile). A threatened teammate city
is assigned to the nearest non-owner member on the same landmass; helpers march
through the existing defense chain. Hierarchy preserved: own threats > committed
offense > teammate help; the city reserve never leaves home. Recomputed each
round, so captured/relieved cities drop out by themselves.

|4 — Solidarity economy
|Locked teammates are always `'alliance'` status, which SKIPS the courtship
scoring block — so the team flows live in their own top-level block in
`AIDiplomacyPolicy.scoreCandidates`: knowledge converges from the leading
member to the trailing one (score 16+, mood-independent; teammates are never
*charged* — `aiTechDealTerms` skips the sale branch), broke teammates get
bootstrapped below `RECONCILE_GOLD_FLOOR` (now shared via `GameConstants`),
trade agreements are proposed (routes pay double), and `mayDeclareWar` blocks a
member from opening an independent second front while the team is at war.

|5 — UI
|Diplomacy modal: Team Commands (attack/defend pickers through the command
seam — `TEAM_COORDINATE_ATTACK` / `TEAM_DEFEND_CITY` with full turn/ownership/
team authorization), Team War Plan panel, "Team" chip in the Observations feed.
Statistics modal: Teams tab (totals, per-member ledgers, knowledge spread,
exploration %, war plan, defense missions) — FFA-inert. 34 i18n keys × 6
languages (24 `gm.diplo.team.*`, `gm.diplo.observations.team`, and the 9
`stats.tabTeams` / `stats.teams*` labels).

|6 — Verification
|`scripts/run-team-batch.mjs` (see §3).
|===

== 3. Verification batch

`node scripts/run-team-batch.mjs [gamesPerConfig] [rounds] [outJson]`

Runs paired seeds (7, 19, 33) through three configurations — free-for-all
(baseline), 2v2 `[0,1,0,1]`, 3v1 `[0,0,0,1]` — on the 40x40 `AI_VS_AI` map
via the canonical `makeEngine`/`runTurns` harness (real fog of war, so claims
and war plans are actually exercised). Sample run (3 games × 150 iterations ≈
up to ~450 rounds each, or until game over):

[cols="2,1,1,2,2,2,2,2", options="header"]
|===
|config |games |captures |plan convergence |claim yields |tech spread (avg) |broke-member rounds (<80g) |defense directives / saved

|FFA  |3 |6  |—            |0            |—      |0*  |—
|2v2  |3 |13 |**93%**      |~0 (max 2/game) |6.57 |519 |15, 5/8
|3v1  |3 |5  |45%          |~0 (1 total) |5.90   |253 |6, 1/3
|===

\* FFA has no teams, so team-scoped counters are zero by construction.

=== Findings

* **Convergence works.** With a joint primary set, 93% of member bulk plans
  (2v2) hit it — two AIs siege the same city instead of two different ones.
  3v1 is weaker (45%) because the 3-member team spreads local plans and the
  solo enemy often dies before the plan stabilises (2/3 games ended by round 55).
* **Team wars are decisive.** Paired seeds: 13 captures (2v2) + 5 (3v1)
  versus 6 in free-for-all — team wars break the historical stalemate
  (the 17-wars-in-465-rounds symptom).
* **Scout claims are a rarely-needed guard, by design.** Claim yields hover at
  ~0 and the starvation fallback fired once in nine games. The probe explains
  why: a scout's local ring is always *its own* claim (distance 0–1 to itself),
  and per-civ scout zones already separate scouts within a civ — the filter
  only fires when two scouts physically cluster, which the region system
  itself prevents. Claims compute correctly (64–247 cells with two scouts on a
  team; zero once a team drops to one explorer). See §5.
* **Defense fires under real pressure.** 21 directives across the team games;
  6 of the 11 tracked cities still stand with their owner at game end — the
  rest died with wars that ended in elimination (notably the 3v1 solo civ).
* **No economic collapse.** Zero bankruptcies in all nine games (FFA: also 0);
  broke-member rounds (519/253) show the pressure exists and the bootstrap has
  something to do. min gold across all games: 12.
* **Knowledge converges slowly.** Within-team tech spread stays near 0 early
  (0 → ~1 at round 50) but grows late (2v2 → 11.5, 3v1 → 30.5 by the last 25
  samples): one gifted technology per pair per 5-round diplomacy pass cannot
  keep up with diverging late-game research paths. The mechanism works; the
  cadence is the knob to turn if tighter parity is wanted.

=== Caveats

* Runs are **seed-pinned for the map but not bit-identical**: unit ids embed
  `Date.now()`, so tie-breaks (`hullChoiceIndex`, army-group ids) drift between
  executions. Paired configurations share maps and RNG streams, not full
  trajectories. (This is server-split blocker #2 — deterministic ids.)
* Round counters in the runner are `runTurns` iterations (~2–3 game rounds
  each); "150 rounds" in the runner's header means iterations, actual games ran
  up to ~450 rounds.
* `yields` are sampled per iteration, not per round — intermediate rounds'
  counters reset in `processRound` before the sample; treat them as lower
  bounds.

== 5. Honest gaps / follow-ups

* **Claim yields ≈ 0** — verified correct but rarely triggered in current
  games (see §3). If scout clustering ever reappears (e.g. after the naval
  pivot puts more hulls on the water), the guard is in place and its fallback
  is starvation-proof; the batch will show yields climbing.
* **Tech-gift cadence** — consider a shorter diplomacy interval for trailing
  teammates, or gifting on demand when the gap exceeds N.
* **3v1 convergence (45%)** — the odd-member-out runs local plans; a
  front-assignment nudge for surplus members is the natural next step (it is
  also where pincer scripting belongs — TODO Next Step 4).
* **Defense survival metric** counts cities still owned at game *end*, which
  conflates "mission failed" with "the whole war was lost"; a per-directive
  10-round survival check would be sharper.
* The batch runner's own metrics: war/invasion log keys did not match the
  current diplomacy log lines (captures are the reliable activity signal);
  fix the keys when touching the script.
