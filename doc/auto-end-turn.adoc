= Auto. turn ending — how it works

The *Auto. turn ending* setting automatically ends the player's turn once
every one of their units has finished acting, so the player does not have to
press *End Turn* manually.

== Where the setting lives

* UI checkbox: `src/components/ui/SidePanel.tsx` — *"Auto. turn ending"* in the
  side-panel header.
* Game state: `settings.autoEndTurn` (default `false`) in
  `src/stores/GameStore.ts`.

== When auto-end fires

Auto-end fires only when *all* of the following hold (`GameEngine.checkAndEndTurnIfNoMoves`
`src/game/engine/GameEngine.ts`, then `EngineEventHandlers.onCheckAutoEndTurn`
`src/utils/EngineEventHandlers.ts`):

1. The setting is enabled (`settings.autoEndTurn === true`).
2. No *active* unit has moves left. A unit is considered *done* (does not
   block auto-end) when any of these is true:
   * `movesRemaining <= 0`
   * it is *sleeping*
   * it is *fortified*
   * it was explicitly *skipped*
   * it is flagged `areTurnsDone`
3. The turn queue is empty (every unit processed / removed from the queue).
4. No screen is open that must block it:
   * *Any open store dialog* defers auto-end while it is open — the guard is
     `EngineEventHandlers.isDecisionScreenOpen`
     (`EngineEventHandlers.ts:2174`): everything except the two ids in
     `GameModals.NON_BLOCKING_DIALOGS` = `['help', 'tech']`
     (`GameModals.tsx:158`). City management (`city-details`,
     `city-production`), `diplomacy`, `village`, rates, government,
     statistics, pause, `research-required`, … all defer; the check is re-run
     when the dialog closes (`GameModals.handleCloseDialog`,
     `GameModals.tsx:160`). Closing `tech` re-runs the check too (its id is
     in the "non-blocking" list but is explicitly re-checked), closing `help`
     does not.
   * The ids `city-purchase`, `city-citizens`, `diplomacy-report` and
     `hex-details` are **not** dialog ids — they are absent from the
     `activeDialog` union in `types/game.ts:878` — so nothing defers "while
     they are open" because they cannot be open.
   * A *combat animation* defers auto-end while playing; the check is re-run
     **1370 ms** later — `duration` 800 + `deathFadeDuration` 450 + 120 ms,
     the `setTimeout` in `EngineEventHandlers.onCombat`
     (`EngineEventHandlers.ts:1066,1078-1084`).
   * The *WORLD* menu (and GAME / INFO) is not an `activeDialog` at all: it
     is App state (`activeMenu`, `App.tsx:63`) rendered by `GameMenuSheet`,
     so it never defers auto-end. Neither does the *tech tree* or *help* —
     the only two ids that do not defer. The *"No Research Selected"* prompt
     (`research-required`) is an ordinary blocking dialog: it defers while
     open, and the research gate below defers the turn regardless of which
     screens are up (see 5).
5. A *technology is selected for research* — or nothing is left to research.
   While the human has no `currentResearch` and researchable techs remain, the
   turn is NOT auto-ended. Once per gap an informational *"No Research
   Selected"* modal (`ResearchRequiredModal`, dialog id
   `research-required`) is shown (`EngineEventHandlers.onCheckAutoEndTurn`,
   `researchPromptedForGap`): it points out that science is idle and offers
   *Choose Technology* (opens the tech tree) or *Decide Later* — it never
   force-opens the tree. If the player ends the turn anyway, a random
   available technology is auto-selected (`GameEngine.autoSelectResearch`
   called from `TurnManager.endHumanTurn`) so science never sits idle.
6. GoTo movement paths are not currently being processed
   (`TurnManager.isProcessingGoTo` guard).

== When the check runs

`checkAndEndTurnIfNoMoves` is evaluated after every unit action that can finish
a unit:

* a unit moves (`moveUnit`)
* a unit attacks another unit (`moveUnit` → `combatUnit`)
* a unit attacks a city (`moveUnit` → `resolveCityCombat`)
* a unit skips (`skipUnit`)
* a unit sleeps (`unitSleep`)
* a unit fortifies (`unitFortify`)
* a settler founds a city (`foundCityWithSettler`)
* a worker builds an improvement (`buildImprovement`)
* a worker cleans pollution (`cleanPollution`)
* a diplomat performs an action (`executeDiplomatAction`)
* a unit is disbanded (`disbandUnit`) or attached (`attachUnit`)
* a city-management dialog closes (`GameModals.handleCloseDialog`)
* the UNIT_MOVEMENT phase starts, after GoTo paths are processed (`TurnManager`)

== What happens when it fires

* The engine emits `CHECK_AUTO_END_TURN` → `onCheckAutoEndTurn` re-checks the
  setting and screen guards → asks the player to confirm by dispatching
  `showEndTurnConfirmation`.
* The App shows the *"All Your Units Have Moved!"* modal with **[Cancel] /
  [End Turn]** — the player can cancel (e.g. to wake a unit or adjust a city)
  or confirm to end. With `skipEndTurnConfirmation` enabled the turn ends
  immediately (truly automatic). Confirming ends the turn via
  `TurnManager.endHumanTurn()` through CITY_PRODUCTION → RESEARCH → END → next
  player.
* Before prompting, the engine logs a skipped-turn summary to the console
  (e.g. `Auto-end summary — skipping turn, units skipped: warrior(skip), settler`),
  and after an auto-ended turn the App shows a brief recap notification
  (e.g. `Auto-end: 2 units skipped: warrior, settler`).
* When the setting is *off*, no auto-end occurs; instead, when the queue
  empties during the human's UNIT_MOVEMENT phase, a one-time
  *"All Your Units Have Moved!"* prompt is shown (per turn).
* AI players also auto-advance when their queue is empty and no active units
  with moves remain.

== Turn queue is the source of truth

Auto-end only triggers when the *turn queue* is empty. Every action that
spends or destroys a unit's movement must therefore remove that unit from the
queue before the auto-end check runs:

* `skipUnit` → `unitDone`
* `unitSleep` / `unitFortify` → `checkUnitStatus`
* `foundCityWithSettler` / `attachUnit` → `removeUnit`
* `disbandUnit` → `unitDone`
* `moveUnit` (combat), `moveUnit` (city combat), `buildImprovement`,
  `cleanPollution`, `executeDiplomatAction` → `checkUnitStatus` / `removeUnit`
  (regression fix; previously the spent unit stayed in the queue and blocked
  auto-end)
